SMART BIN INTELLIGENCE

Historical observations. Transparent predictions.

The prototype uses a trained HistGradientBoosting model and engineered time-series features. Its estimate is on a 0–10 scale and extends the last historical observation by one calendar observation.

AI WASTE ANALYSIS

Image-based assessment, not invented certainty.

Gemini Vision analyzes only the evidence visible in a submitted image. The service validates image content and returns a structured assessment with an explanation and recommended action.

!

Provider access and availability can vary. When analysis is unavailable, the console reports the issue instead of fabricating a result.
